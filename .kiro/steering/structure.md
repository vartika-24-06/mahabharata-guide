# structure.md

- backend/          FastAPI app (main.py, routers/, middleware/, adapters/, tests/)
- frontend/         React + Vite app (src/components/, src/state/, src/modes/)
- e2e/              Playwright tests (optional)
- docs/             provider-verification-notes.md, decision log, case-study notes
- .kiro/steering/   product.md, tech.md, structure.md
- .kiro/specs/      one folder per feature (requirements.md, design.md, tasks.md)

Later features (corpus, retrieval, Q&A, story mode) add their own folders
under backend/ and frontend/src/. Source text and embeddings stay out of git;
they are regenerated by a script.
